Enumerate original indices, shuffle with `random.Random(42)`, save the entire order, then inspect every hit if at most 300 or the first 120 otherwise. The largest source is 53.9%; every proposed scenario remains at most four. This is quota arithmetic, **not a selected or accepted batch**.
